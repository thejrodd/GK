Welcome

A Brotherhood Built on Achievement

Welcome to the Gamma Kappa Chapter of Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. We are a chapter defined by our unwavering commitment to scholarship, leadership, and service — values instilled in us from the day our fraternity was founded in 1911.

Our brothers represent some of the most dedicated, accomplished, and community-minded men in our region. We believe that excellence is not an event but a habit, and that brotherhood is not a title but a lifelong covenant.

Whether you are a prospective member, a community partner, or a fellow Kappa, we invite you to explore what the Gamma Kappa Chapter stands for — and what it means to be a man of Kappa Alpha Psi.
