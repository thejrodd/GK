Our Foundation

A Legacy of Brotherhood

Founded January 5, 1911 at Indiana University, Kappa Alpha Psi Fraternity, Inc. stands as one of America's most distinguished Greek-letter organizations — built on achievement, service, and brotherhood.

The Gamma Kappa Chapter

The Gamma Kappa Chapter carries the torch of Kappa Alpha Psi's storied legacy with distinction. Our brothers represent the highest ideals of scholarship, leadership, and civic responsibility — forging bonds that last a lifetime.

We are united not by chance but by choice — drawn together by a commitment to excellence and an unwavering dedication to our communities, our families, and one another.

Our Identity

The Four Pillars

Every brother of the Gamma Kappa Chapter embodies these foundational principles — passed down through generations of Kappas.

Scholarship

Academic excellence is the cornerstone of our brotherhood. We hold each other to the highest intellectual standards.

Brotherhood

The bond between Kappas is unbreakable — forged through shared sacrifice, trust, and a lifelong commitment to one another.

Community

Service to our communities is not optional — it is who we are. We uplift, mentor, and invest wherever we go.

Leadership

Kappas lead with integrity and vision. We develop men who shape industries, communities, and generations.
