National Program

Guide Right Program

The Guide Right Program is Kappa Alpha Psi's nationally recognized mentorship initiative, dedicated to developing the intellectual, social, and leadership potential of young men. Through workshops, mentorship pairs, and community engagement, Guide Right has positively impacted thousands of youth across the nation.
