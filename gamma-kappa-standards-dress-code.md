# Gamma Kappa Standards — Informal Meeting

## Meeting Discussion Points

1. Welcome and purpose of the meeting — review chapter standards as a refresher, not a punishment session.
2. Recap: why standards matter — chapter image, sisterhood accountability, and school/community perception.
3. Walk through the new Dress Code Rules & Regulations (below) line by line so everyone hears the same version.
4. Walk through the Event Attendance policy — especially the "identify ahead of time" and "email Adams directly" requirements.
5. Explain the reasoning behind requiring members to email Adams themselves (accountability, no relayed excuses, clear record).
6. Set the effective date for the policy.
7. Open floor — questions, concerns, edge cases (e.g., what counts as "mandatory," what if a conflict comes up last-minute).
8. Confirm who members should contact with questions after the meeting (Adams, and/or Standards Chair).
9. Wrap up — thank everyone for attending, remind of next standards check-in date.

## Dress Code Rules & Regulations

### General
- These standards apply to all active members unless a documented exception is granted in advance.
- "Dressed up" means neat, put-together, chapter-appropriate attire — no pajamas, gym clothes, or clearly unkempt outfits on designated days.
- Letters (when worn) should be clean and presentable.

### Pretty Tuesday
- All members are expected to dress up for Pretty Tuesday.
- Appropriate attire: dresses, skirts, nice tops, or dressy casual outfits — put together, not thrown on.
- Hair and makeup should be neat and intentional (simple is fine — the standard is "put together," not "formal").
- Sneakers/athletic wear, sweatpants, and pajama-style clothing do not meet the standard for this day.

### Well Dressed Wednesday
- All members are expected to dress up for Well Dressed Wednesday.
- Appropriate attire: business casual or dressy outfits — think "put together for a good impression," not costume-level formal.
- Same grooming standard as Pretty Tuesday — neat and intentional.
- Same restrictions apply: no athletic wear, sweats, or pajama-style clothing.

### Enforcement
- Standards Chair (or designated officer) may do a quick visual check on Pretty Tuesday / Well Dressed Wednesday.
- Repeated non-compliance without a valid, pre-communicated reason may result in standard chapter accountability measures (per bylaws).

## Attending Mandatory School Events

1. **Identify ahead of time.** If you know you have a conflict with a mandatory school event, you must identify that conflict *before* the event — not after the fact.
2. **Email Adams yourself.** Members must personally email Adams to report a conflict or request an excused absence. No exceptions for having someone else (a sister, a friend, a group chat message) pass along the excuse on your behalf — it has to come directly from you.
3. **Do this early.** Notify Adams as soon as you're aware of the conflict, not the day of the event.
4. **Keep a record.** Since the request goes through email, it's documented — that protects both you and the chapter if attendance questions come up later.
5. **No response ≠ approval.** An absence isn't excused until Adams confirms it. Don't assume — follow up if you haven't heard back.
